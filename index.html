Our brand in three words

1Warm

Welcoming, never corporate.

Playful, because humans and collaboration matter.

3Wise

Simple and clear, so hard topics feel easy.

Warm

Cream paper, soft pastel washes and friendly faces make everything we put out, from slides and the website to courses and posts, feel welcoming, never corporate.

  • Paper, not white screens
  • Pastel washes, not flat corporate colour
  • We talk like a person, not a press release

Whimsical

Wobbly ink, marker lettering and friendly doodles balance the mechanical, systems-heavy ideas in our trainings with a playfulness that reminds everyone that humans and collaboration matter.

“Factories, pipelines, agents… and people, working it out together.”

Wise

Simplicity and clarity first, with one idea at a time, steps in a clear order and simple, familiar images, so even hard topics are easy to understand.

1One idea

One idea per slide, section or post.

2In order

Numbered steps that read in order.

3Familiar

Everyday objects to explain abstract things.

4Plain words

Words normal people use. Jargon only when needed, and explained clearly and concisely.

The wobbly line

Every outline wobbles, but only a little: just enough to feel drawn by a person, never messy.

  • Thin lines: bubbles, panels, arrows, doodles
  • Chunky lines: character outlines
  • Never turn the wobble up
“Drawn by a person, not a machine.”

Pictures: objects first

To explain an idea, draw a familiar thing: thin wobbly ink over a watercolour wash. Use one colour for simple objects and up to three when an object has distinct parts, always from the palette. New objects are welcome.

Characters: supporting cast

A chunky clean outline with a pastel wash.

  • Small and at the edge, never the centrepiece
  • Always doing something: reading, waving, pointing
  • A mix of people who look like our learners
  • Groups show collaboration; the cat and dog add a smile

Do & don't

Do
  • One idea at a time
  • Use the kit's pieces and colours
  • Explain with everyday objects
  • Leave space: paper is part of the look
Don't
  • Stock photos or glossy gradients
  • New colours or fonts
  • Big cartoon mascots front and centre
  • Extra wobble for “more fun”
