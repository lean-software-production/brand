Example slide — style reflected from the “How to learn anything w/ an Agent” lightning-lesson slides

What I could actually see (and what I borrowed from it)

  • Hand-drawn ink cartoon — thick black outlines, flat muted fills, little “burst” tick marks for emphasis, cheeky characters (the “What happened at the meeting you missed?” comic).
  • Titles — heavy black marker caps sitting on a pale-yellow highlighter swash.
  • Frameworks — numbered, solid-colour circles with same-colour marker-caps labels, slate arrows between steps, a dashed box around the highlighted step, and a big blue curved arrow looping back (the “framework strip” used on every “learn a book” slide).
  • Infographics — cream paper, ribbon banner, rust vs. deep-teal panels, small doodle icons beside each bullet.
  • The slide’s text is the lesson’s own: the 5-step loop from the strip, plus the “learn a book while driving / Ch1 of Shape Up” insight from the learn a book 1 frame.

Caveats, honestly: the originals are AI-generated raster illustrations — I’ve redrawn the look in SVG, so the characters here are rougher than the real ones. The fonts are my nearest Google Fonts matches (Luckiest Guy / Patrick Hand SC / Patrick Hand), not identified from the slides. About half the lesson frames are all named “FOO”, and the CLI couldn’t pull their images one at a time, so I may be missing some slides.
